# About Arcadia

**Welcome to Arcadia — a community built around modding, creativity, and unforgettable gaming experiences.**

Arcadia is a gaming and modding community dedicated to creating, sharing, and improving carefully crafted modlists for some of our favorite games. Our goal is to bring together players, modders, and enthusiasts who share a passion for transforming their games into something extraordinary.

## Our Modlists

We develop and maintain custom modlists designed around distinct themes, gameplay experiences, and creative visions.

Our projects include:

- **Arcadia Everwild** — A high-fantasy Skyrim experience filled with breathtaking wilderness, exploration, enhanced gameplay, and new adventures.
- **Arcadia Sands** — A Fallout 4 overhaul featuring a sun-scorched frontier atmosphere, expanded settlements, improved combat, and new adventures.
- **The Arcadia List AE** — A comprehensive Fallout 4 experience combining expanded content, improved gameplay, and a carefully assembled collection of modifications.
- **Dreadfall** — A dark, horror-focused Fallout 4 experience built around psychological horror, terrifying creatures, and survival.

Each project has its own identity, with dedicated configurations and carefully selected modifications.

## Our Community

Arcadia is more than just a collection of modlists. It's a place for people to connect, share their experiences, exchange ideas, and enjoy gaming together.

Our community offers:

- Modlist support and troubleshooting.
- Guides, documentation, and installation resources.
- Community discussions and recommendations.
- Multiplayer gaming and community events.
- A place to share creations, screenshots, and gameplay.

## Our Philosophy

We believe modding should be about creativity, experimentation, and building experiences worth sharing.

Every modlist represents countless decisions, testing, adjustments, and improvements. We aim to provide experiences that are enjoyable, thoughtfully assembled, and continuously evolving.

We also recognize the work of the mod authors whose creations make these projects possible. Their contributions are an essential part of the modding community.
