# Arcadia Everwild
## Getting Started Guide
*The Player's Handbook for a Reimagined Skyrim*

Welcome to **Arcadia Everwild**, a heavily modded Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition experience built around exploration, adventure, challenging combat, survival, immersive environments, and a vast collection of quests.

This is not simply vanilla Skyrim with better graphics. The world contains expanded locations, new lands, additional followers, new equipment, redesigned interiors, seasonal environmental changes, and a major museum collecting system.

This guide will help you begin your adventure, understand the important gameplay systems, and avoid common mistakes during your first hours.

---

## 1. Before You Begin

### Launching the game

1. Open Mod Organizer 2.
2. Select your Arcadia Everwild profile.
3. Launch Skyrim through the profile's configured **SKSE launcher** rather than launching Skyrim directly through Steam.
4. Be patient the game can take a while to launch, its loading alot of plugins.
4. Start a **new game** with your current mod configuration.
5. Allow the game to finish initializing its mods before beginning your adventure.

### Important: Be patient on your first launch

Arcadia Everwild includes Legacy of the Dragonborn, a large museum and quest expansion that performs initialization when a new game begins.

- Allow the museum to finish configuring itself.
- Do not repeatedly reload or restart because initialization takes a while.
- Watch for the notification confirming that the museum has finished its initial setup.
- Give other mod configuration menus time to appear before changing settings.

The initial Legacy of the Dragonborn setup can take several minutes. Starting from an existing save made with a different mod configuration is not recommended.

## 2. Choosing Your Beginning

Arcadia Everwild includes **Alternate Start – Live Another Life**, allowing you to begin outside the traditional Helgen opening.

Choose a start that suits the character and story you want to play.

### Recommended starting approach

For a first playthrough, choose a relatively straightforward start that gives you access to ordinary shops, beds, food, and basic equipment. Learn the survival and combat systems before venturing into dangerous territory.

If you want to make the museum a central part of your adventure, consider a start that places you in Solitude or lets you travel there early.

### Your early objectives

During your first few hours, aim to:

- Acquire a dependable weapon and suitable armor.
- Find a reliable source of food and drinking water.
- Discover a town where you can rest and resupply.
- Learn how your survival and combat settings affect your character.
- Visit the Legacy of the Dragonborn museum in Solitude.
- Begin exploring nearby areas before committing to long expeditions.

You do not need to rush into the main quest. Arcadia Everwild supports a more open-ended adventure.

## 3. Survival: Food, Water, Sleep, and Cold

Arcadia Everwild uses **SunHelm Survival** to add survival mechanics.

Your character must manage hunger, thirst, fatigue, and exposure to cold. Depending on your settings, diseases and other survival effects can also become important.

### Practical survival advice

**Food:** Carry enough food for your journey. Long expeditions without supplies can leave you vulnerable.

**Water:** Keep drinking water available. Check your inventory before leaving settlements and replenish your supply when possible.

**Sleep:** Rest regularly rather than waiting until you are exhausted. Inns and safe sleeping locations are useful stops during travel.

**Cold weather:** Pay attention to the weather and your surroundings. Snowy mountains, freezing conditions, and long outdoor journeys can be dangerous.

**Camping:** Campfire is installed, giving you camping-related options for wilderness travel. Use it as part of your preparation when venturing far from settlements.

### A good travel routine

Before leaving town:

1. Eat and drink.
2. Make sure you have enough food and water for the trip.
3. Check your equipment and carry weight.
4. Plan where you will rest.
5. Avoid taking on a long expedition when your needs are already in poor condition.

You can customize SunHelm through its available settings menu. If survival feels too punishing or too forgiving, review the settings rather than immediately adding another survival overhaul.

## 4. Combat and Character Progression

Combat is enhanced by **Wildcat**, while **MorrowLoot Ultimate** changes the equipment and loot experience. **Experience** also changes how character progression works.

Expect to be more deliberate about equipment, positioning, and preparation than in an unmodded game.

### Combat tips

- Do not assume every enemy is appropriate for your current level.
- Carry healing supplies and keep an escape route in mind.
- Use blocking, movement, and opportunities to recover instead of trading blows indefinitely.
- Check your armor and weapon choices before entering a dungeon.
- Avoid carrying every piece of equipment you find; your supplies and loot compete for inventory space.

### Building your character

Your list includes **Ordinator**, **Apocalypse**, **Odin**, **Triumvirate**, and **Forgotten Magic Redone**.

These additions expand perk choices and magical possibilities.

Choose a general direction for your character early on, such as:

- Warrior — melee combat, armor, and weapon specialization.
- Ranger — bows, exploration, and wilderness skills.
- Rogue — stealth, lockpicking, and opportunistic combat.
- Mage — spellcasting and magical specialization.
- Hybrid — a combination of combat and magic.

You can experiment, but spreading your resources across too many unrelated abilities early on may make difficult encounters harder.

## 5. The Legacy of the Dragonborn Museum

One of Arcadia Everwild's major features is **Legacy of the Dragonborn**, which turns collecting and displaying artifacts into a substantial part of the adventure.

The museum is located in Solitude. It provides a long-term reason to explore dungeons, complete quests, discover unique objects, and revisit locations.

### Getting started

1. Travel to Solitude.
2. Visit the museum.
3. Speak with the curator and follow the available introduction.
4. Complete the initial requirements to become involved with the museum.
5. Begin collecting unique artifacts and quest rewards.

The museum can be approached naturally as you play. You do not need to abandon every other activity to focus on it immediately.

### Become a collector

As you explore, pay attention to:

- Unique weapons and armor.
- Quest rewards and named artifacts.
- Rare books and historical objects.
- Items associated with major adventures.
- Treasures discovered in new lands.

Do not assume that every valuable-looking object is a museum display item. Follow the museum's instructions and use its display tracking systems where available.

### Why the museum matters

Your modlist includes numerous large quest expansions. The museum gives your adventures a common purpose: discover more of the world, recover its treasures, and build a collection over time.

For official information, visit the [Legacy of the Dragonborn mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/11802).

Arcadia Everwild includes many adventures beyond the original Skyrim questlines.

- Hunt dangerous targets.
- Explore dungeons for treasure.
- Sell unwanted equipment.
- Collect artifacts for the museum.
- Visit shops and improve your equipment.

Your list includes **Opulent Thieves Guild**, alongside **JK's Thieves Guild HQ**, **JK's The Ragged Flagon**, and related compatibility patches.

## 9. Seasons and the Environment

Arcadia Everwild includes a substantial collection of environmental and visual modifications.

Your active list includes **Seasons of Skyrim**, **Seasonal Landscapes**, **Seasonal Landscapes – Unfrozen**, **Turn of the Seasons**, **Witchy Wilderness Seasons**, **Ulvenwald**, and seasonal weather components.

These mods support an environment that can change with the seasons, alongside redesigned landscapes, vegetation, lighting, and weather.

### What to expect

- Different environmental appearances across seasonal conditions.
- Extensive changes to trees, plants, landscapes, and other scenery.
- Modified interiors and city spaces.
- Additional environmental detail and visual effects.

The exact appearance and timing depend on the installed configuration and seasonal settings. Not every object necessarily has a seasonal variant.

### Performance advice

- Give the game time to load when entering demanding areas.
- Avoid immediately adding more graphics mods if you have not tested performance.
- If you encounter unusual missing objects, terrain seams, or seasonal visual problems, investigate the existing configuration before installing more environmental overhauls.

## 10. Alchemy, Crafting, and Equipment

Your list includes **Apothecary**, **Gourmet**, **Harvest Overhaul Redone**, **Honed Metal**, **Trade Routes**, **Common Clothes and Armors**, **Armor Variants Expansion**, **Immersive Weapons**, and other equipment additions.

These mods broaden the equipment and item experience and change aspects of crafting, alchemy, trading, and the economy.

### Helpful habits

- Keep useful ingredients rather than selling everything immediately.
- Examine potion effects and recipes instead of assuming they work exactly as they do in vanilla Skyrim.
- Visit blacksmiths and merchants when upgrading your equipment.
- Use Honed Metal's services where appropriate if you prefer to commission equipment improvements.
- Sell excess equipment to maintain a manageable inventory.
- Explore different armor and weapon options before settling on a permanent build.

Apothecary changes the alchemy experience, so review ingredient effects and potion recipes as you encounter them.

## 11. Common First-Session Questions

### Why are some mod configuration menus taking time to appear?

Some mods initialize after a new game begins. Allow the initial setup to finish and check again before assuming something is broken.

### Why am I getting weaker during travel?

Check SunHelm's hunger, thirst, fatigue, and cold indicators. Your character may need food, water, sleep, or protection from cold conditions.

### Why is an enemy so difficult?

Wildcat and MorrowLoot Ultimate contribute to a more demanding combat and loot experience. Consider retreating, improving your equipment, or exploring somewhere less dangerous.

### Why does an item seem important?

It may be a quest item, a unique artifact, or an object intended for the Legacy of the Dragonborn museum. Read item descriptions and follow quest instructions before discarding unusual items.

### Can I add or remove mods during a playthrough?

Treat the modlist as a complete setup. Changes to plugins, quests, worldspaces, and compatibility patches can affect an existing save. For significant changes, make a backup and follow the relevant mod's installation guidance; a new game may be required.

### What should I do if the game crashes?

1. Record what you were doing when the crash occurred.
2. Check whether the crash happens consistently in the same location or situation.
3. Review any available Crash Logger report.
4. Check for missing masters, plugin warnings, or incompatible changes.
5. Avoid randomly disabling multiple mods in an active save to try to fix the problem.

Make one change at a time and test methodically.

## 12. Recommended First Few Hours

If you are unsure where to begin, use this simple route.

**Phase 1 — Establish yourself**
- Choose your alternate start.
- Finish the initial mod setup.
- Find basic equipment and supplies.
- Locate a safe place to rest.

**Phase 2 — Learn the systems**
- Test your combat style against manageable enemies.
- Learn how to monitor food, water, sleep, and cold.
- Visit merchants and familiarize yourself with the equipment available.

**Phase 3 — Visit Solitude**
- Find the Legacy of the Dragonborn museum.
- Complete its introduction.
- Learn how artifacts and displays are tracked.

**Phase 4 — Begin exploring**
- Take on a few local jobs or bounties.
- Explore nearby dungeons and points of interest.
- Collect unique items and useful supplies.
- Recruit a companion if you want one.

**Phase 5 — Choose your first major adventure**
- Continue the main Skyrim storyline, pursue a guild, or follow a lead into one of the new quest expansions.
- Avoid committing to the most dangerous content before you are ready.
- Let your character's interests determine the next destination.

## Final Advice

Arcadia Everwild is intended to reward curiosity and preparation. You do not need to complete every quest, collect every artifact, or discover every new land immediately.

Travel at your own pace, take care of your character, explore the world, and let the adventure develop naturally.
